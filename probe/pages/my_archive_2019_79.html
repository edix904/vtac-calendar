Principal dates for Monash University Malaysia (2019)

This section outlines important University dates including:

  • Semester dates and holidays
  • Important events and deadlines

Consider highlighting those dates which apply to you for easy reference.

* Asterisk indicates entry subject to change.

The following information is applicable to new and returning students enrolling in Semester One and/or Semester Two 2019.

New students who commence in October 2019 need to refer to the principal dates for October 2019 intake.
