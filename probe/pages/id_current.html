Principal dates

This principal dates page serves as a concise guide to all essential timelines throughout the academic year, offering crucial information on public holidays, university close, and the overview of academic calendar.

Tue 16 University closed: Muharram / Islamic New Year
Sat 20 Final assessments end: Monash Indonesia term 2 (MI-T2-6)
July
Mon 06 Results released: Monash Indonesia term 2 (MI-T2-6)
Mon 20 Teaching starts: Monash Indonesia term 3 (MI-T3-6)
Mon 27 Teaching starts: Monash Indonesia Semester 2 (MI-S2)
Fri 31 Graduation applications close: November round (Indonesia)
August

Mon 17 University closed: Indonesian Independence Day
Tue 25 University closed: The Prophet Muhammad's Birthday
Mon 31 Census date: Monash Indonesia Semester 2 (MI-S2). Last day to withdraw from units without incurring fees and Withdrawn showing on your academic record
September
Sat 05 Teaching ends: Monash Indonesia term 3 (MI-T3-6). Last day to withdraw from units with Withdrawn Fail showing on your academic record (units cannot be withdrawn after this date)
